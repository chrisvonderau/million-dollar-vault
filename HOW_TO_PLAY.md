# The Million-Dollar Vault — How to Play

**Answer trivia. Reveal letters. Solve the vault. Reach the prize first.**

Two contestants race at the same time on one iPad lying flat between them. Each player has their own half of the
screen (the far half is turned to face the far player), their own questions, their own hidden phrases and their own
clock. Nobody waits for a turn. Your half only reacts to your touches.

## Before the match (v2)
- **Who's playing?** Tap a name box and type with the on‑screen keyboard (or a hardware keyboard). Names are remembered.
- **Handicap badge** for each player, so a kid and an adult can have a fair race:
  | | Easy | Normal | Hard |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Letters per correct answer (Standard / Challenge) | +2 / +3 | +1 / +2 | +1 / +2 |
  | Questions | Standard‑level pool only | all | all |
  | Written vault hint unlocks at | 50% of letters | 75% | 85% |
  | Wrong‑solve cooldown | 3 s | 5 s | 7 s |
  | Showdown answer time | 9 s | 6 s | 5 s |
  | Showdown buzzer | live at once | live at once | arms when 60% of the letters are showing |
  In simulations an Easy‑badge child vs a Hard‑badge adult wins about half the time (see ACCEPTANCE.md).

## The race
1. **Countdown.** Both players start together after a shared 3‑2‑1.
2. **Pick a level and a category.** *Standard* reveals **1 letter** of your vault phrase when you answer correctly.
   *Challenge* questions are harder and reveal **2 letters**. Then tap one of three categories.
3. **Answer.** A correct answer flips letters in your vault. A wrong answer shows you the right answer and a short
   explanation for about 1.5 seconds. There is no penalty, and you go straight back to picking.
4. **Solve the vault** whenever you think you know the phrase. You don't need every letter. Tap **SOLVE** and type the
   answer on your own QWERTY keyboard (keys are at least 44 px). Letters already revealed are locked in gold; tap a typed letter
   to remove it. *⌫* and *Clear* fix mistakes, and *Trivia* takes you back to questions without losing your work. **Submit** checks it:
   - Correct: the door swings open and your next, harder vault appears.
   - Wrong: a 5‑second solve cooldown starts. You can keep answering trivia while it runs.
5. **Vault hint.** Each vault shows its category and letter count. Once 75% of its letters are revealed, a written hint
   unlocks. The *Insider Clue* prize unlocks it right away.
6. **Prize wheel** after doors 2 and 4. **Flick or drag the wheel** (finger, mouse or trackpad; a harder throw spins longer), or tap the big **SPIN** button, or press Space/Enter. Where it lands is decided fairly at random, never by how hard you throw; the pointer stops exactly on that wedge. The 24 wedges: **cash** for your Vault Bank (bragging rights only, shown on the results screen; it never decides the winner; land the throw meter in the **PERFECT** zone and cash is doubled), **prizes** (below), one **JACKPOT** (choose any prize), one **VAULT LOCKED** (lose your stored prize and your Vault Bank) and one **FROZEN 4s** (a 4-second pause before the next vault). Already holding a prize? Press **Keep** (`K`) to skip the spin; landing on a different prize asks whether to swap. If nobody spins, a hint appears after about 8 s, a reminder at 14 s, and the wheel spins itself after 20 s (if you already hold a prize, you keep it):
   - **Extra Letter**: reveal one more letter whenever you like.
   - **Fifty‑Fifty Lens**: remove two wrong choices from a question.
   - **Insider Clue**: show this vault's hint now, or a second, extra clue if the hint is already showing.
   You can hold **one** stored prize. Winning another asks whether to replace it. Tap your prize slot to use it.
7. **Five doors**: Lobby Safe, Gallery Vault, Diamond Chamber, Laser Vault, and The Million‑Dollar Vault. Open the fifth
   door first to stop the race and take a **1‑point head start** into the Showdown. Finishes within a quarter of a second
   count as a tie, and both players start the Showdown on 0.
8. **The Million‑Dollar Showdown** (two players only). One shared phrase clue appears on both sides, and its letters
   flip over one by one. **Buzz** when you know it. Only the player who buzzed sees 4 answer choices, on their own side.
   - Right answer: +1 point. **First to 2 points wins the vault.**
   - Wrong answer or out of time: that buzzer is locked out for 3 s and the rival can **steal**. A player who has missed
     twice in a round can't buzz again that round.
   - Taps that land in the same frame are settled by the exact touch time, not by who the code saw first.
   - After 8 rounds (or if the clue pool runs out) the higher score wins; level scores are a shared victory.
9. **Results** show both players' Showdown points, times and stats. Choose *Rematch* (new puzzles) or *Menu*.

## Family Scoreboard
Every finished two‑player match is recorded by name: wins, matches, best vault time and win streaks. The reigning
champion wears the 👑 on the title screen. *Scoreboard → Reset* asks for confirmation before clearing it. Solo games
are not recorded.

## Family Pack (your own questions and phrases)
*Settings → Family Pack → Edit…* lets you add, edit and delete your own trivia questions (question, 4 answers, the
correct one, an optional explanation) and vault phrases (phrase, clue, optional extra clue). The pack starts with a
small starter set, and the **Include** switch puts a *Family* category in the game's offers. *Move pack* saves a
`.mdvpack` file or shows a pack code (`MDVPACK1:…`) for moving the pack to another device. See IPAD_INSTRUCTIONS.txt.

## The host
Your host is the vault's ancient, very polite keeper: a deep, slow British voice with a faint echo, a dry gothic‑butler wit and nothing actually scary. He reacts to big moments with short recorded lines, shown as captions too (placed so they never cover a header or the letter tiles). In two‑player
games the host never reads anyone's private questions aloud; it only reads the shared Showdown clue. In Solo it can
read your question. *Settings → Host voice mode*: **Clips** (recorded lines; this device's own voice reads questions),
**Device voice** (everything in the device's built‑in voice), or **Captions only** (silent). Pausing stops the host.

## Controls & options
- **Pause** (centre button) pauses both players. The game also pauses itself if the iPad is locked or you switch apps.
- **Settings**: host voice on/off, host voice mode and volume, read questions aloud, Family Pack, two‑player layout (*Facing* = tabletop, far half turned around; *Same direction* = sitting side by side), music, effects and master volume, mute, reduced motion, and match length
  (*Standard* 5 doors, *Quick* 3 doors).
- **Solo Practice** (great on a MacBook) uses the mouse or keyboard. Once you press a key or use a mouse, small key hints appear on the buttons:
  | Key | Action |
  |---|---|
  | `1` `2` `3` | pick a category |
  | `D` or `Tab` | toggle Standard / Challenge |
  | `A`–`D` or `1`–`4` | answer |
  | `S` | open the solve panel. Type letters, `Backspace` undoes, `Enter` submits, `Esc` goes back to trivia |
  | `U` | use your stored prize |
  | wheel: `Space`/`Enter` spin (Space never pauses here; `Esc` does), `K` keep, then `Y`/`N` swap or `1` `2` `3` JACKPOT choice | flick or drag the wheel, or tap SPIN; tap Keep / Swap / a JACKPOT prize |
  | `Space` / `P` / `Esc` | pause / resume (`Esc` closes the solve panel first) |
  | results: `Enter` / `R` | rematch (`Esc` for menu) |

A typical two‑player game lasts about 9–13 minutes including the Showdown (Quick: about 4–7).
